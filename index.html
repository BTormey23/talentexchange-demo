Share capacity across the enterprise

Employees with authorized spare capacity list their skills and hours. Teams facing a volume surge borrow trained, onboarded colleagues instead of opening a new-hire req. The borrowing team pays the same share of salary as the share of time borrowed.

1 · Create profile

Resume, systems you know, and skill levels.

2 · Set availability

Weekly time windows, share of your week, and the engagements you'll take: PTO coverage, seasonal surge, or customer/project surge.

3 · Supervisor sign-off

Your direct supervisor approves the hours, cap and end date.

4 · Get matched

Area managers and above search by skill and system after SSO sign-in. The borrowing cost center pays the same % of your salary as the % of time borrowed.

Try the demo flow

Each tab is a different user's view. Walk through them in order:

  1. My Profile — Maria completes her profile and submits it.
  2. Supervisor Approvals — her supervisor approves with a cap and end date.
  3. Find Talent — sign in through SSO. Try a team supervisor (blocked) and then an area manager (can search). Maria now appears in results.
  4. Program Dashboard — the enterprise view of hours shared and cost avoided.

Guardrails built in

  • No listing goes live without the direct supervisor's approval.
  • Supervisor sets a hard cap on % of time and an expiry date; can revoke any time.
  • Borrowing managers agree not to recruit the people they borrow, during the engagement or for 6 months after. Employees who want a permanent move can say so on their profile, with their supervisor's approval.
  • Employees reconfirm availability every 30 days; supervisors re-approve every 90 days. Lapsed listings drop out of search.
  • Home team keeps first call on the employee's time.
  • Profiles are visible only to area, state and region managers, senior executives and HR, through SSO. Peers and line supervisors cannot browse them.
  • Chargeback = employee's salary × % of time borrowed, moved from the borrowing cost center to the home cost center.
  • Access, licensing and data rules checked before assignment starts.
