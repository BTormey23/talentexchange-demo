The problem

Volume doesn't move evenly. Our staffing does.

Rates, seasons, PTO and big portfolio deals hit different teams at different times. In the same week, one team is idle and another is posting a req.

Slow teams carry idle cost

When a market cools, trained people sit under-used. The P&L carries them, or we cut and lose the know-how we'll need again in six months.

Busy teams hire for peaks

A portfolio deal, a seasonal spike or a wave of PTO triggers new-hire reqs that take weeks to fill and ramp. They often arrive after the peak and stay after it ends.

Skills are stuck behind walls

Expertise sits inside brands, divisions and regions. A manager in Dallas can't see the under-used Ignite expert in Philadelphia.

The fix

A marketplace for approved spare capacity

Employees with real spare time list what they know and when they're free. Their supervisor approves how much can be lent and for how long. Leaders facing a surge search across the company, borrow by the hour, and pay the same share of salary as the share of time they use.

  • No new headcount, recruiting fees or onboarding
  • Lending teams recover cost; borrowing teams pay about half the cost of a new hire
  • Employees build skills across divisions and stay visible
  • When the surge ends, people go back to their home team

What the technology is

You're protected

Your job, your pay, your choice

  • Your pay, benefits and title don't change. The borrowing team pays your home team, not you.
  • You choose. You confirm every request and can decline any of them.
  • Home team first. Your supervisor approves and can recall you if volume returns.
  • Private by default. Only area managers and above, and HR, can see your profile, after SSO sign-in.
  • No poaching. Borrowing managers sign a no-poach agreement. A permanent move only happens if you ask for one.
  • Easy to pause. You reconfirm every 30 days and can pause your listing any time.

Questions people ask

Will I work more hours?

No. Borrowed hours come out of your normal week, up to the % your supervisor approves. If you're paid hourly, overtime rules still apply. HR to confirm the policy.

What if I don't like the assignment?

Tell your home supervisor. Either side can end an engagement early, and you go back to your home team.

Who can see my pay rate?

Area managers and above, and HR, because chargeback is based on salary. HR may choose to show pay bands instead. HR policy to confirm.

Does this count in my review?

Verified skills go on your record. HR decides how reviews and promotion use it.

Can my supervisor say no?

Yes. Their team's work comes first, and they know the workload best. You can ask again when volume changes.

Your commitments as a borrower

Fair play keeps lenders lending

  • No poaching during the engagement or for 6 months after, unless the employee has opted in to a permanent move. Window to be set by HR.
  • Respect the cap, the time windows and the employee's time zone.
  • Keep the home supervisor informed, and release the person early if their team needs them back.
  • Verify skills at the end. It takes two minutes and builds the employee's record.

Manager questions

What if my volume comes back mid-engagement?

Recall them. The demo design gives the borrower notice, and the home team always has first call.

Who manages the work day to day?

The borrowing manager directs the work. The home supervisor stays the person's manager for HR purposes.

How does the money move?

Monthly: salary × % borrowed, from the borrowing cost center to the lending cost center. Finance to confirm the mechanics.

What about system access and licensing?

Access is provisioned per engagement. Licensed work is checked against the employee's licenses before it starts. IT and Compliance own these checks.

For P&L owners

Flex capacity without adding headcount.

Talent Exchange turns idle payroll in one unit into productive capacity in another. The cost to the borrowing unit is about half that of a new hire, and the net cost to the enterprise is roughly zero, because the salary is already being paid.

The P&L logic

Cost moves inside the company instead of leaving it

Borrowing unit

Pays salary × % borrowed, about $33/hr on a $68K salary, compared with about $66/hr all-in for a new hire. No recruiting, ramp-up or exit cost.

Lending unit

Is credited the same salary share for hours it was paying for anyway. Margin improves in exactly the quarters it's under pressure.

Enterprise

Net zero between units, so the external cost of the hire, its ramp-up and its exit is avoided. Benefits stay with the lending unit; Finance can add a load factor.
